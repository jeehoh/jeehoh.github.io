Firstly, why I shouldn't pursue a PhD:

- I am incredibly selfish for pursuing a PhD when I have responsiblities as the head of the household.
- My decision to pursue a PhD will put my wife's career on hold and in jeapordy.
- Doing a PhD will probably be the worst financial decision I will ever make.
- There's no gurantee that I will become a scientist even after I earn my PhD.

But why I want to pursue a PhD:

- I enjoy the process of learning something and discovering that there's much more that I don't know than I do know.
- I like a good and well-thought out explanations to *why* something works the way they do, so I want to get more practice in explaining what I learned to other people.
  - This practice might come in the form of teaching a class, writing a good research paper, or presenting research findings at an academic conference.
- I learned (through my undergraduate research experience) that I really enjoy talking about research with like-minded people, so I want to be in an environment where I can be with like-minded people.
- The more I learn about the world through research, the more I realize how undeniable God's existence is.

- Practical reasons:
  - I learned that it is very difficult to do PhD-level work while also having a "real job." So, I pursue a PhD to dedicate a part of my life to do PhD-level work.
  - To become a "scientist" in the real world usually requires me to pursue higher degrees like a PhD.
